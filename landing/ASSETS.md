Eye level view through wide rectangular doorway into open living dining kitchen space. Restrained, lived-in but impeccably clean, no people, no text, no logos, no computer or phone, no graphics. Plenty of spatial depth, grounded realistic proportions. This is a standalone mood photograph, not a website screenshot.
